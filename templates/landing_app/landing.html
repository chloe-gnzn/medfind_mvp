About

MedFind is a web platform that connects people with pharmacies. Search for a medicine by brand or generic name, see which verified pharmacies carry it, compare prices, check stock status, and see whether the pharmacy is open before you leave home.

Search for a medicine, see which verified pharmacies have it in stock, and compare prices and hours. Pharmacies update their own inventory, and every one is reviewed by an admin before it appears.

WhatIt Does

ItsPurpose

MedFind aims to make that search quick and reliable by putting pharmacy availability in one place, while helping local pharmacies reach more customers.

Note: MedFind shows medicine availability only and does not provide medical advice. Always consult a licensed pharmacist or doctor, and note that prescription medicines require a prescription.
